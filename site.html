Where'd You Get That?, A Unique Consignment Store
Where'd You Get That? entered a new era of ownership in 2023. What started as a thoughtful reimaging has evolved into a refined boutique known for its curated selection of upscale women's fashion, accessories, and distinctive home furnishings. The store was honored as Best Consignment Store in Beaufort in both 2024 and 2025 and recognized among South Carolina's Top 10 Consignment Stores - a testament to its commitment to quality and discovery. Today, Where'd You Get That? is more than a consignment store. It is sought-after shopping destination where sophisticated coastal aesthetic meets curation, and every piece tells a story of style. At the heart of the store is the commitment to both consignors and customers - and to the exceptional team that makes it all possible.
Consign. Discover. Style Your Home and Your Wardrobe.

About Us

  • At Where'd You Get That?, we believe beautiful homes and wardrobes don't have to come from brand-new stores. The store is a thoughtfully curated consignment destination where style, sustainability, and discovery come together.
  • Built on strong relationships with the community, local consignors trust us to showcase their pieces, while shoppers enjoy the thrill of finding something distinctive at an exceptional value. With new arrivals daily, every visit offers the possibility of uncovering a treasure you didn't know you were looking for.
  • We sincerely appreciate your trust, your support, and the opportunity to serve our community.
